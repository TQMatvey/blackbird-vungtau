# Black Bird active design
Approved 2026-11-02. This design is the active production source. The previous version is preserved on archive/classic-2026-11-02.

Direction: a food-led counter menu. Crimson hero with a real menu photograph, cream menu, dark checkout. Be Vietnam Pro, square controls, clear rules. Desktop uses two columns of dishes within full-width category sections; mobile uses one column. Inline Add and size steppers replace the duplicated picker. Selected items move into a receipt with contact fields. A floating order total offers a direct checkout link. Timeline, stale rating, old address explanations and internal sourcing copy are omitted.

Truth: existing catalog and image mappings copied from current project; whole Napoleon is 560000 VND. No invented delivery terms or preparation times. Checkout sends to the existing Telegram Worker. Bank QR and app selection are shown only after the Worker returns payment details. Sent means forwarded to the shop, not accepted or paid. English, Vietnamese and Russian are supported. Locale changes preserve cart and contact values. The slogan stays English in every locale.
